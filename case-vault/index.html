Fintech · B2B payments

NovaPay — cutting onboarding drop-off by half

A payments platform losing 62% of sign-ups before first transaction needed a diagnosis deeper than UX polish.

Problem

NovaPay's self-serve onboarding averaged 14 steps across KYC, bank linking, and API key setup. Product analytics showed a cliff at step 7 — business verification — where users abandoned without error messages. Sales blamed "bad leads"; support logged hundreds of "stuck" tickets monthly.

I ran 18 moderated sessions and mapped the flow against competitor benchmarks. Root cause: verification requirements surfaced late, after users had invested 12 minutes. Trust eroded; completion felt uncertain.

Solution

We resequenced onboarding around progressive disclosure: eligibility check upfront, estimated time-to-live, and a "save & resume" state persisted across devices. Partnered with compliance to pre-fill known business data via registry APIs where legally permitted.

Shipped a status dashboard showing exactly what was pending and who internally was blocking (NovaPay ops vs. bank partner). Reduced perceived opacity — the emotional driver of drop-off.

Result

  • 48% increase in completed onboarding (31% → 46%)
  • −37% support tickets tagged "onboarding stuck"
  • 11 days → 4 days median time to first transaction

Case documented internally; referenced in two enterprise sales cycles that closed within the quarter.

Health tech · HIPAA

Helix Health — shipping a compliant patient portal on deadline

A regional clinic network needed a patient-facing portal in six months — with legal, security, and clinical stakeholders who rarely agreed.

Problem

Helix had burned nine months on a vendor build that failed penetration testing. Engineering wanted to rebuild; legal wanted to pause; clinicians wanted lab results and messaging yesterday. No shared definition of MVP — every feature was "critical."

Audit logs were inconsistent; PHI exposure risk was real. The board set a hard launch date tied to a payer contract. Failure meant a seven-figure penalty clause.

Solution

I facilitated a risk-ranked backlog workshop: must-have for launch vs. phase-two. Defined a compliance gate checklist co-signed by legal and the CISO — no feature shipped without passing it.

Introduced weekly "evidence reviews" — demo recordings, test results, and decision logs — so stakeholders saw progress without attending every standup. Negotiated scope trade-offs: deferred appointment scheduling to phase two; prioritized secure messaging and lab results.

Result

  • Launched on day 178 of 180-day window
  • Zero critical findings in post-launch audit
  • 84% patient activation within 60 days (target was 70%)

Portal became the template for two additional clinic rollouts; internal case study adopted by the parent health system.

SaaS · Revenue

Lattice CRM — redesigning pricing without churning the base

A mid-market CRM needed to move from seat-based to usage-based pricing — without triggering the backlash their competitor had faced six months prior.

Problem

Lattice's ARPU had flatlined for eight quarters. Power users subsidized idle seats; finance modeled a 22% revenue lift from usage tiers but feared churn among agencies with seasonal staffing. Sales had no talk track; customer success had no migration playbook.

Competitive intel showed a rival's botched rollout — public pricing page changes overnight, no grandfathering — dominated G2 reviews for months.

Solution

Ran conjoint analysis with 240 customers across segments. Identified three tiers anchored on workflow automation credits, not raw seats. Built a 90-day grandfather window and a "price lock" offer for annual contracts.

Created role-specific enablement: CS got migration scripts and ROI calculators; sales got objection handlers backed by pilot data. Soft-launched to 8% of accounts with a feedback loop before full rollout.

Result

  • +19% NRR within two quarters (target +15%)
  • 2.1% logo churn during migration (model predicted 4–6%)
  • Net Promoter +6 vs. pre-launch baseline

Pricing framework reused for an adjacent analytics add-on; case presented at company all-hands as the standard for major GTM changes.
